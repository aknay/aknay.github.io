Privacy Policy for SG NextBus Arrival Effective Date: October 24, 2026 1. Overview This Privacy Policy explains how SG NextBus Arrival (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles user data. By using the app, you agree to this policy. Please note that this app utilizes public transport data provided by the Land Transport Authority (LTA) DataMall API but is an independent application not officially affiliated with or endorsed by LTA. 2. Information We Collect The app does not require user accounts or registration. Location Data If you grant location permissions, precise device location (GPS coordinates) is processed locally on your device solely to find nearby bus stops, calculate distances, and display your relative location on the map. Location coordinates are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. Device and Usage Data Device model and operating system version App interactions (such as bus stop searches, button taps, and screen views) App performance data (crashes, errors, diagnostics) Device or other identifiers (such as Firebase installation ID) Advertising Data Google Advertising ID (used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and measurement) 3. How We Use Information To fetch and display real-time bus arrival timings and nearby stop information using the LTA DataMall API To improve app performance and stability To understand usage trends and improve user experience To display and measure advertisements To diagnose and fix technical issues 4. Third-Party Services The app uses third-party services that may collect and process data: Land Transport Authority (LTA) DataMall API (for public transport schedules and real-time arrival data) Google Analytics for Firebase Firebase Crashlytics Google AdMob These services process data according to their own privacy policies. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell personal data. Some automatically collected diagnostic or advertising data may be shared with third-party service providers for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising purposes. Location data remains strictly on your device and is never shared with third parties. 6. Data Retention and Deletion SG NextBus Arrival does not require user accounts and does not store personal data or location logs on its own servers. Data Retention Period Data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill analytics and advertising purposes, in accordance with Google's standard retention policies (typically ranging from 14 to 50 months), or until a manual deletion request is processed. Data Deletion Requests In compliance with Google Play’s Data Safety and User Data policies, users have full control over the erasure of their data. If you would like to formally request the deletion of analytics, performance, or diagnostic data associated with your device, please contact us directly at maker.dev.lab@gmail.com. Upon receiving your request, we will verify your device/advertising identifier and initiate a deletion request with our third-party service providers (Google Firebase and Google AdMob) to permanently purge the data from their systems. All manual deletion requests will be fully processed and completed within 30 days. You may also stop all future data collection and delete locally stored app data immediately by uninstalling the app from your device. 7. Security We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. 8. Children's Privacy This app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. 9. Your Choices & Data Control You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings. You may disable personalized ads in your device settings. You may reset or limit your advertising identifier through your device settings. To formally request data erasure across our third-party networks, please email us at maker.dev.lab@gmail.com. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days. You may uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection. 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
